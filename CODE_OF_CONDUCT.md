# Code of Conduct

Issues and discussions in this repository are for Signal Console users and
Intrusion Academy staff to work together on the software.

- Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and
  discriminatory language are not accepted.
- Keep to the subject of the issue.
- Never post data from a host you investigated, credentials, or details that
  identify a person or an organisation.
- Never post instructions for attacking systems.

Intrusion Academy moderates this repository and may edit, hide or remove
content, and block accounts, that do not follow this code. To report a
problem, contact Intrusion Academy through <https://intrusion.academy/signal>.
